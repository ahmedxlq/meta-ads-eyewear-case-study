# Meta Ads Case Study: Vision Jeddah Eyewear 🕶️

**Hey there!** 👋 

I’m Ahmed, and I’m building a portfolio of real-world marketing strategies to transition into Paid Media. Since I don't have a client budget to spend yet, I created this "Ghost Project" to show exactly *how* I think and *how* I would spend a client's money.

## 📌 The Concept
I invented a fictional eyewear brand based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia called **"Vision Jeddah"**. 
*   **The Goal:** Drive direct e-commerce sales. 
*   **The Budget:** $500/month.
*   **The Catch:** The target audience is in Saudi Arabia, so the ads *must* be bilingual. 

*(You can click on the `Marketing.pdf` file in this repository to see the full 4-slide pitch deck).*

## 🎯 How I Targeted the Audience
I didn't want to waste money showing sunglasses to people who don't shop online. 
*   **Location:** Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.
*   **Demographics:** Men & Women, Ages 18–35.
*   **Interests:** Fashion, Eyewear, Luxury Brands, Shopping.
*   **Placements:** Instagram Feed & Stories (Using Advantage+ to let Meta's AI find the cheapest clicks).

## ✍️ The Creative Strategy
When designing the ad, I followed one main rule for the Saudi market: **Arabic first, English second.** 
The ad uses a striking black-and-white image with neon green text ("20% OFF") to stop people from scrolling. I added "Free Delivery inside Jeddah" because local shipping offers are a massive conversion booster in e-commerce.

## 📊 The Math (Forecasting)
If a client gave me $500, here is exactly what I would expect to happen:
*   **Estimated Cost Per Click (CPC):** $0.50
*   **Total Clicks:** 1,000
*   **Expected Conversion Rate:** 2% (A standard benchmark for e-commerce)
*   **Total Sales:** 20 Orders
*   **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):** $25 per order. 
*(If the sunglasses cost $50+, this campaign is highly profitable for the brand).*

## ⚙️ How I Would Track It
If this went live, I wouldn't just guess if it worked. I would install the **Meta Pixel** on the website to track "Add to Cart" and "Purchase" events. This feeds data back to the Facebook algorithm so it learns exactly what kind of people are buying, allowing us to scale the budget later.
